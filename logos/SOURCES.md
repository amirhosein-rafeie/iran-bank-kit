Vector originals and the Snapp Store MIT license are retained in `source/`.
The 28 Snapp Store vectors use a 48 × 48 viewBox; rasterization expands it to
`-3 -3 54 54` to prevent clipping. Rendering used `@resvg/resvg-js` 2.6.2,
`fitTo: { mode: 'width', value: 512 }`. This tool is not a runtime dependency.

Refah artwork: Refah Bank; Wikimedia uploader Mshams1, uploaded 2022-08-22.
The Commons page cites the bank's [original SVG](https://www.refah-bank.ir/UI/Styles/Refah1400/images/logo.svg).
Direct retrieval from the bank was unsuccessful on 2026-10-22, so this is the
documented 2022 redesign, not an independently verified claim of today's branding.
`source/refah-original.svg` preserves the complete wordmark; `source/refah.svg`
retains only the three emblem paths and original gradients, with square viewBox
`185 -5 80 80`. The derived emblem and PNG retain attribution to the original;
see [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

All 30 existing logos were visually compared with candidate vector artwork.
`pasargad.png` is retained because its inherited gold color is more legible on
the demo’s light background than the bright yellow candidate. Its original
source/license provenance remains unresolved. No logo was added for the three
institutions that previously had no asset.

This is a source and rendering review, not issuer confirmation of current brand
guidelines. Direct checks of Sepah, Karafarin and Pasargad websites were
unsuccessful. Historical Ansar, Kosar and Ayandeh assets remain historical; bank
succession is still resolved by the detector. These source licenses do not grant trademark rights or imply bank
endorsement, and the package's MIT code license does not relicense bank marks.
